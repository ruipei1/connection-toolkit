The idea behind the toolkit

Care can be there.
And still go unseen.

Understand the empathy perception gap ↗

We can underestimate how caring our peers are—and miss chances to connect.

Research from the Stanford Communities Project explores how these expectations shape social life, and how learning about others can help us reach out.

From an idea to an everyday action

Notice. Get curious. Reach out.

Notice your prediction.

What do you imagine would happen if you started a conversation or asked for support?

Consider another possibility.

Separate what you’ve observed from what you’ve inferred. What else could be true?

Take a manageable step.

Make an invitation, offer appreciation, or check in. Reflect on what you learn.

You don’t need a perfect opening.

Just a small, thoughtful action—and room to see what happens.
