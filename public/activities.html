Modules & activities

A small experiment
in connection.

Start with the time and setting you have. Each activity invites curiosity about other people, while leaving room for different experiences.

Group activity

Expectations vs. reality

◷ 15 minutesPairs or small groups

Compare expectations about peers with your own willingness to connect.

Group activity

More than one story

◷ 10–15 minutesPairs or small groups

Explore plausible interpretations of an ambiguous social moment.

Solo activity

One small step

◷ 5–10 minutesSelf-guided

Turn a social assumption into a specific, manageable action.

Solo activity

A week of reaching out

◷ 5 minutes + follow-upSelf-guided

Choose a small challenge and reflect on what actually happens.

These short activities adapt ideas from the supplied workshop and program outline. They are educational activities; their individual effects have not been evaluated.
