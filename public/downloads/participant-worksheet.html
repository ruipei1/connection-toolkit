FROM PERCEPTION TO CONNECTION

My next small step

Work with an everyday situation. You can skip any prompt. Keep this worksheet for yourself.

1. What small action am I considering?

2. How do I imagine they might respond?

3. What have I observed? What else could be true?

4. What will I say or do? When?

Afterwards

What happened? What surprised me? What did I learn?

Keep your step manageable. Respect the other person’s response. One interaction does not determine every future connection.
