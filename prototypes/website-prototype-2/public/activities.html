More than one story · Group

A classmate declines lunch without suggesting another time. Privately write your first interpretation. In pairs, list plausible alternatives, including less positive ones. Separate what you observed from what you inferred. What evidence would help? Sharing is optional.

One small step · Individual

Choose one person and one action: invite them to study, say thanks, or check in. Write what you expect and when you will try it. Respect their answer. Afterward ask: What happened? What surprised me? What remains uncertain?

A week of reaching out · Individual

Choose one small invitation or expression of appreciation this week. Set a day. Afterward compare your expectation with the experience. One interaction does not describe every future connection.

Make participation and personal disclosure optional. Activities are educational adaptations; their individual effects have not been independently evaluated.
