FROM PERCEPTION TO CONNECTION

Three ways to get curious

Expectations vs. reality · 15 minutes · Groups

Privately rate your own willingness to welcome a conversation, then estimate your peers’ willingness. Compare reasoning in pairs, with sharing optional. Discuss how expectations shape action. Group self-reports are not guarantees of future behavior.

More than one story · 10–15 minutes · Groups

Consider: “Everyone is looking at their phone when you enter class.” Privately note your first interpretation. In groups, generate plausible alternatives. Separate observations from inferences. Ask what evidence supports each story and what small action might help you learn more.

A week of reaching out · 5 minutes plus reflection · Solo

Choose one: invite a classmate for a meal, thank someone, check in with a friend, or ask to study together. Write a specific plan and an expectation. After you try it, compare your prediction with what happened. You may learn from a disappointing interaction as well as a welcome one.
