Rate your own receptiveness. Estimate classmates’ receptiveness. Then let the group’s reports add new information.

Prepare / collect / discuss

Ask first. Reveal later.
Leave room for surprise.

01 · Collect privately

Open a session and share the participant link or QR code. Allow about 3 minutes to answer before discussing the science. Participation is optional.

02 · Close & compare

Close collection to freeze results. Select which group charts to present. Compare reported receptiveness with estimates using the same 0–100 scale.

03 · Reflect & act

What stands out? Where were estimates close? How might expectations affect reaching out? Choose one assumption you could test this week.

A gap does not have to appear. Discuss the patterns you actually get. These are self-reports about hypothetical situations, not guarantees about how a particular person will respond.
Timing and preparation

15–20 minutes total: 3 minutes for private responses, 5 minutes to compare results, 5–10 minutes for discussion and a small next step. Prepare a display and participant devices. Offer an alternative if someone cannot use a device.

What does the survey ask?

Four paired situations: starting a conversation, inviting someone to coffee, receiving appreciation, and listening to an ordinary difficult day if you had time. Students rate their own receptiveness and estimate the average classmate’s receptiveness on a 0–100 scale.

Participant privacy

No names or emails are requested. The organizer can export individual rating responses; the presentation shows only selected group summaries after at least three responses. Keep the organizer screen private. Explain your retention plan and delete sessions when no longer needed.
