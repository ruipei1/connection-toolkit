Everyday connection · 3 minute read

Make the next step smaller.

A small reflection on expectations, curiosity, and connection.

By [Author name]

“Make more friends” is a large, uncertain goal. “Ask a classmate whether they want to study after class” gives you something specific to try.

Name the action and the moment.

Choose what you will say, to whom, and when. Keep the action manageable and leave the other person room to choose.

Reflect on what you learn.

Afterward, compare your expectation with what happened. What surprised you? What remains uncertain? One interaction gives you information about that moment, not every future connection.
