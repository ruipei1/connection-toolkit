About the program

to connection.

A toolkit for building social connection in college.
[Program lead name] & [Program lead name]

The transition to college is full of possibilities. Students arrive hoping to find friends, feel understood, and become part of a community. Their peers often want the same things. Yet these shared hopes can remain invisible.

This program translates research on the empathy perception gap into practical opportunities for students to examine expectations and practice reaching out. Campus educators and community leaders can use the resources in orientation, residence life, and first-year courses. Students can also explore the ideas independently.

Curiosity, with room for uncertainty.

The goal is to help students recognize promising opportunities and feel prepared to act on them. Relationships involve uncertainty, and some interactions will be disappointing. There is no need to dismiss those experiences to stay open to new ones.
