A self-guided activity · 5–10 minutes

What’s one assumption
you could test?

Start with an everyday situation. You can skip a prompt, and you never need to share something personal.

Your answers stay on this page and are cleared when you reload. Nothing is submitted.

1. Notice your expectation.

2. Leave room for another story.

An expectation is a prediction. Consider what you know, what you’re inferring, and what another explanation might be.

3. Make the next step concrete.

Keep it manageable and respect the other person’s answer. Afterwards, ask yourself: What did I predict? What happened? What did I learn?

Your next step

Small is a good place to start.

After you try it

Notice what happened, including anything unexpected. A single response is information about that moment, not a verdict on every future connection.
